# Happy Detailers 🚗✨

## Website Project

Happy Detailers is a modern and responsive car washing and vehicle detailing website. The website is designed to provide customers with information about the business, its services, pricing, gallery, booking options, enquiries, and contact details.

The website focuses on creating a professional online presence for Happy Detailers while making it easy for customers to find services and request a booking.

---

## About Happy Detailers

Happy Detailers is a fictional professional car detailing business based in Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa.

The business provides vehicle cleaning, detailing, polishing, and protection services for customers who want their vehicles to look clean, fresh, and well-maintained.

### Services Offered

- Exterior Car Wash
- Interior Vacuuming
- Interior Deep Cleaning
- Upholstery Shampoo
- Paint Polishing
- Waxing
- Ceramic Coating
- Engine Bay Detailing
- Full Vehicle Detailing

---

## Website Features

The Happy Detailers website includes the following pages:

### Home Page

The home page introduces Happy Detailers and provides customers with quick access to the main services, booking page, and contact information.

### About Page

The About page provides information about Happy Detailers, the company's approach to vehicle detailing, and why customers can choose the business.

### Services Page

The Services page displays the different detailing services offered by Happy Detailers together with descriptions and pricing information.

### Gallery Page

The Gallery page displays photographs of vehicle cleaning and detailing work, including before-and-after examples.

### Booking Page

The Booking page allows customers to enter their details and request a vehicle detailing appointment.

### Enquiry Page

The Enquiry page allows customers to submit questions about services, prices, availability, or other information.

### Contact Page

The Contact page provides the business contact details, location information, and a map showing the Johannesburg area.
